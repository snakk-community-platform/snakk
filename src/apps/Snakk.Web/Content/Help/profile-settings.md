Your profile settings let you customize how you appear on Snakk and control your account preferences.

## Accessing settings

Click **Settings** in the sidebar, or go to `/settings` directly.

## What you can change

- **Display name** — how others see you across the platform
- **Bio** — a short description shown on your profile
- **Avatar** — upload a custom image or keep the generated one
- **Timezone** — used for displaying dates and times
- **Skin tone** — preference for emoji display

## Settings tabs

Settings are organized into tabs:

- **Profile** — display name, bio, avatar
- **Display** — timezone, theme preferences
- **Notifications** — control what notifications you receive
- **Privacy** — visibility and data preferences
- **Integrations** — connected accounts and embed preferences
- **Account** — password, two-factor authentication, account deletion
