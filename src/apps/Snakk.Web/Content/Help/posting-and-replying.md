Every discussion on Snakk is a conversation. After reading the opening post, you can add your reply using the editor at the bottom of the page.

## Replying

Click the reply area to open the markdown editor. Write your response and click **Post**. Your reply appears immediately in the discussion.

You can also reply to a specific post by clicking the reply icon on that post. This creates a threaded reply that's visually linked to the post you're responding to.

## Editing

After posting, you can edit your reply by clicking the edit button (pencil icon) on your post. The edit history is preserved — other users can see that a post was edited.

## Reactions

You can react to any post with emoji reactions. Click the reaction button to see available reactions. Reactions are a lightweight way to agree, thank, or respond to a post without writing a full reply.

## Formatting

Posts support markdown formatting. See the Markdown Guide for the full syntax reference.

## Images

You can upload images directly into your post by dragging them into the editor or using the image button in the toolbar. Images are automatically optimized and thumbnailed.
