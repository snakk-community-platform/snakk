Rules help communities maintain a healthy environment. Snakk supports rules at every level of the hierarchy.

## Rule levels

- **Community rules** apply to everything in the community
- **Hub rules** apply to all spaces within the hub
- **Space rules** apply to discussions in that specific space

When viewing a space, you see the combined rules from all three levels. Space-level rules appear alongside the inherited community and hub rules.

## Viewing rules

Click **Rules** in the sidebar to see the rules for your current context. Rules are always visible before you post.

## Enforcement

Moderators enforce rules through the moderation tools: warnings, content removal, temporary bans, and permanent bans. If you believe content violates the rules, use the report button to flag it for moderator review.
