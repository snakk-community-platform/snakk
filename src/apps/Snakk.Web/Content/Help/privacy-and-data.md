Snakk gives you control over your privacy and how your data is used.

## Privacy settings

Visit **Settings > Privacy** to configure:

- **Profile visibility** — who can see your profile information
- **Activity visibility** — whether your recent activity is shown on your profile
- **Online status** — whether others can see when you're active

## Your data

Snakk stores:

- Your account information (email, display name, avatar)
- Your posts and replies
- Your votes, reactions, and follows
- Session data for authentication

## Embed preferences

Some content on Snakk includes embedded media from third-party providers (YouTube, Spotify, etc.). You can control which providers are allowed to load in **Settings > Integrations**. When a provider is disabled, you'll see a placeholder instead of the embed.

## Account deletion

You can delete your account from **Settings > Account**. This removes your profile information. Your posts may be retained with your display name anonymized, depending on community settings.
