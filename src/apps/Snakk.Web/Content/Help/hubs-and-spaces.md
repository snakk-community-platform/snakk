Within each community, content is organized into hubs and spaces.

## Hubs

Hubs are broad categories that group related spaces. A gaming community might have:

- **PC Gaming** (hub)
  - Hardware (space)
  - Game Recommendations (space)
  - Tech Support (space)
- **Console** (hub)
  - PlayStation (space)
  - Xbox (space)
  - Nintendo (space)

Hubs don't contain discussions directly — they organize spaces.

## Spaces

Spaces are where discussions live. Each space:

- Focuses on a specific topic
- Has its own moderators (in addition to community and hub moderators)
- Controls which discussion types are allowed
- Can have its own rules (in addition to community-wide rules)
- Shows a discussion list, follower count, and activity stats

## Navigating

Use the breadcrumb trail at the top of any page to navigate up the hierarchy: Discussion → Space → Hub → Community → Home. You can also browse all spaces within a hub from the hub's page, or explore communities from the homepage.
