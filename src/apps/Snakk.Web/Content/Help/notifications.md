Snakk sends you notifications when activity relevant to you happens on the platform.

## What triggers notifications

- Someone replies to your discussion
- Someone replies to your post
- Someone mentions you
- Someone follows you
- A discussion you follow gets a new reply
- Moderation actions on your content

## Notification settings

Visit **Settings > Notifications** to control which notification types you receive.

## Viewing notifications

Click the bell icon in the navigation bar to see your notifications. Unread notifications are indicated with a badge count. Notifications are delivered in real time via the SignalR connection — you'll see them appear without refreshing.

## Auto-follow behavior

By default, replying to a discussion automatically follows it so you get notified of future replies. You can disable this in your notification settings.
