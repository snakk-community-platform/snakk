Link discussions and some post content include embedded media from third-party providers. Snakk lets you control which providers are allowed to load.

## Supported providers

Snakk supports oEmbed previews from: YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, Twitter/X, Bluesky, Reddit, Spotify, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Twitch, Imgur, CodePen, and Canva.

## Managing preferences

Visit **Settings > Integrations** to toggle individual providers on or off. When a provider is disabled:

- The embed won't load (no network request to the provider)
- A placeholder is shown instead with a button to load it on demand
- Your preference is remembered across sessions

## Privacy considerations

Embedded content loads directly from the third-party provider, which means they can see your IP address and may set cookies. Disabling providers you don't trust prevents this entirely.

## Default behavior

All providers are enabled by default. You can disable any or all of them at any time.
