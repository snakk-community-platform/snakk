If you see content that violates the community rules, you can report it for moderator review.

## How to report

1. Click the three-dot menu (**...**) on any post, discussion, or user profile
2. Select **Report**
3. Choose a reason from the list
4. Optionally add details explaining the issue
5. Submit the report

## What happens next

- The report is sent to the space moderators (or community moderators if the space has none)
- Moderators review the report and take appropriate action
- You'll be notified of the outcome

## Report reasons

Common report reasons include:

- Rule violation
- Spam
- Harassment
- Misinformation
- Inappropriate content

The specific reasons available may vary by community.

## Reporting in good faith

Reports should be made in good faith. Misusing the report system (e.g., mass-reporting someone you disagree with) may result in moderation action against the reporter.

::: callout-warning
Reporting is for rule violations — not for content you simply disagree with. If you have a dispute with another user, reach out to a moderator directly instead.
:::
