You can create a Snakk account in two ways: with an email address and password, or by signing in with an OAuth provider.

## Email registration

1. Click **Sign Up** in the top navigation bar
2. Enter a display name, email address, and password
3. Complete the verification challenge
4. You're in — you can start posting immediately

Your display name is how other users see you. You can change it later in your profile settings.

## OAuth sign-in

Snakk supports signing in with:

- **Google**
- **GitHub**
- **Discord**

Click the provider you want, authorize the connection, and your account is created automatically. If it's your first time, you'll be prompted to choose a display name.

::: callout-tip
OAuth is the fastest way to get started — no email verification required, and you can always connect additional providers later from your profile settings.
:::

## Profile setup

After creating your account, visit your profile settings to:

- Write a bio
- Upload a custom avatar (or keep the generated one)
- Set your timezone
- Configure notification and privacy preferences
