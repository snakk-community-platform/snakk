Snakk uses a hierarchical moderation system where permissions flow downward through the organizational structure.

::: callout-info
Moderation is scoped per community. A moderator in one community has no authority over another community's content.
:::

## Moderator roles

| Role | Scope | Can moderate |
|------|-------|-------------|
| **Global Admin** | Everything | All communities, hubs, spaces, and discussions |
| **Community Admin** | One community | All hubs and spaces within the community |
| **Hub Moderator** | One hub | All spaces within the hub |
| **Space Moderator** | One space | Discussions within that space only |

Permissions flow downward: a Community Admin can moderate anything a Hub Mod or Space Mod can, but not vice versa.

## Moderation actions

Moderators can:

- Remove posts and discussions
- Lock discussions (prevent new replies)
- Issue temporary or permanent bans
- Review and act on reports
- View moderation audit logs

All moderation actions are logged for accountability.

## Transparency

Moderation actions are visible in the moderation audit log. The community's moderator team is listed publicly on the Moderators page, accessible from the sidebar.
