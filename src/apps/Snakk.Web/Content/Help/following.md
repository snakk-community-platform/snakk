Following lets you keep track of discussions, spaces, and users you're interested in.

## What you can follow

- **Discussions** — get notified when new replies are posted
- **Spaces** — get notified when new discussions are created
- **Users** — see their activity in your feed

## How to follow

Click the follow button on any discussion, space, or user profile. The button toggles between followed and unfollowed states.

## Auto-follow

By default, when you reply to a discussion, you automatically follow it. You can disable this behavior in your profile settings under the Notifications tab.

## Managing follows

Your saved and followed content is accessible from the **Saved** link in the sidebar.
