Your profile is your public presence on Snakk. It shows your display name, avatar, bio, activity stats, and recent discussions.

## What's on your profile

- **Avatar** — a unique generated image based on your user ID, or a custom upload
- **Display name** — how others see you across the platform
- **Bio** — a short description about yourself
- **Stats** — your discussion count, reply count, and follower count
- **Activity chart** — a visual history of your contributions over the last 30 days
- **Recent discussions and posts** — your latest contributions, browsable by tab

## Editing your profile

Visit **Settings** from the sidebar to change your display name, bio, avatar, timezone, and other preferences. Your profile updates immediately.

## Following

Other users can follow your profile to stay updated on your activity. You can also follow discussions, spaces, and communities.

## Privacy

You control what's visible on your profile through the Privacy settings. See the Privacy & Data article for more details.
